#### spark-shell On Yarn
```
spark-shell \
    --master yarn-client \
    --executor-memory 1G \
    --num-executors 10
```

#### spark-sql On Yarn
```
spark-sql \
    --master yarn \
    --executor-memory 1G \
    --num-executors 10
```

#### spark-sql显示表头
```
spark-sql \
    --master yarn \
    --executor-memory 1G \
    --num-executors 10 \
    --hiveconf hive.cli.print.header=true
```
